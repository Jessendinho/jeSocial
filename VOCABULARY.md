3. Definitions

- Publication: An individual item of content published by a user or professional source.
- Approval: A positive reaction to a publication.
- Disapproval: A negative reaction to a publication.
- Response: A comment or contribution to a publication's discussion.
- Amplify: Sharing or redistributing a publication.
- The Vault: A user's collection of saved publications.
- Socialize: The action of following another account.
- The Pulse: The main feed of publications.
- The Compass: The discovery centre.
- The Studio: The content creation and publishing centre.
- The Signal: The notification centre.
- My Space: A user's personal profile and account area.

4. Reaction rules

1. A user can have only one active reaction per publication.
2. Approval and Disapproval are mutually exclusive.
3. Selecting the opposite reaction switches the active reaction.
4. Selecting an already active reaction removes it.
5. Reaction counts must reflect actual active reactions.

5. Example interface language

- "Give your approval."
- "Show your disapproval."
- "Write a response."
- "Amplify this publication."
- "Save to The Vault."
- "Explore The Compass."
- "What's happening? Publish in The Studio."

6. Terminology still under review

Socializers: A proposed noun for people who follow an account. This term is not yet final.

Other terminology may evolve, but changes must be approved by the product owner and reflected in this document.

7. Consistency rule

Use the official jeSocial terms in interface labels, documentation, database naming discussions and development.

Do not silently replace these terms with conventional social-media terminology.
